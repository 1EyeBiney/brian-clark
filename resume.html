Summary

Assistive technology instructor and curriculum developer, totally blind since 2015, who uses a screen reader as the sole means of all daily work. Seventeen years as a small-business IT manager, teaching screen readers and mobile accessibility to newly blind adults since 2019 (more than 155 students, 65 of them on JAWS or NVDA), and, since 2025, builder of accessible web applications and local AI pipelines using AI development tools. JAWS Certified since 2019. Salesforce Certified Administrator, 2018 to 2023.

Experience

First Aid for the Blind, Indianapolis. Lead Trainer (independent contractor), 2023 to present

  • Delivered the 25-lesson basic independent-living and technology curriculum remotely to 55 Indiana Vocational Rehabilitation clients; every session recorded and delivered to the student as audio.
  • Wrote the Screen Reader Foundations program: a remote, guided self-study model for Windows screen reader users with four employment tracks (Business Enterprise Program, customer service, higher education, general), a 60 to 90 minute intake assessment, homework, projects, and VR reporting.
  • Built Classtronomical, a pipeline that transcribes each class and produces an accessible study-guide email for the student overnight, using open speech and language models run locally on a laptop processor so that no class recording is sent to an outside AI service; in production since June 2026.
  • Used AI tools to structure the Screen Reader Foundations curriculum from my own teaching notes: program document, track definitions, milestone workbook, chapter class notes, quizzes, and blank templates for homework logs and counselor reports.
  • Built a laptop quote pipeline that searches eBay, Amazon Renewed, Dell Refurbished, and Back Market for refurbished business laptops meeting the program spec, benchmarks them against Computers for the Blind pricing, and writes a Word quote for VR.
  • Built a research pipeline for the agency technology newsletter that gathers, de-duplicates, and verifies assistive technology news for editorial selection; also an instructor class-notes site.

Software built with AI tools, 2026

  • Self-taught audio production in Reaper with AI instruction from 2024: a VoiceOver tutorial podcast, comedy episodes, and AI-generated music and promotional audio.
  • Accessible Bible (accessible-bible.org), Not By Sight (blindnessinthebible.com), Keyboard Commander (keyboard-commander.com), YAY! Accessible Dice, The Silence audio space game: all keyboard-only, screen-reader-first web applications, live.
  • Independent experiment with Symbolic Universal Notation, a symbol-based Scripture system: decoded the published New Testament's 1,306 symbols, statistically glossed 89 percent of its words, and produced an NVDA pronunciation dictionary so a screen reader can speak the symbols.
  • NVDA screen reader add-ons in Python: Favorites Hub (folders, links, snippets, commands, and macros behind a fuzzy quick-pick, in daily use), Speech Harvester (searchable speech history with pattern-based data extraction for moving data between systems, in daily use), File Collector (gathers scattered files for AI review), and Pivot (multi-application workspace launcher with window and focus management).
  • Snappy Play, a keyboard-only Windows audio player in Python with no visual interface and all feedback spoken through NVDA: designed, built, and tested in a couple of hours; starts playing within a fifth of a second of opening a file.
  • Local transcription and language-model pipelines for class notes and podcast promotion; a client-records app for a peer support group.
